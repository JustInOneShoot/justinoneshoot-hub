Privacy

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking of its own, and it has no forms that collect your details.

Email. If you email us, we use your message and address to answer you, deliver your order and follow up about our videos (you can ask us to stop at any time). We don't sell your details. We use AI assistants to help read and answer email and to make videos: your messages may be processed by Anthropic (Claude) and Google, and your photos by our video tools (OpenArt).

Payments. Payments are handled by Stripe on Stripe's own secure pages; we never see your full card details.

Videos. Sample videos may be embedded from YouTube, which may set its own cookies when you play them.

Photos you send. We use your photos only to make your video. We only show your video or name in our marketing if you agree in writing.
